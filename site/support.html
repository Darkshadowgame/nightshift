Signing in

How do I sign in?

Type your email and tap Email me a code. Type the 6-digit code from the email (or tap the link in it on your iPhone). There's no password. New here? The same code makes your account.

The code didn't arrive

Check your junk folder, wait a minute and tap Send it again. Codes last 10 minutes and you can ask for one once a minute.

Can I try it without an account?

Yes. Tap Try the demo without an account. You get sample people and events round your area, and nothing leaves your phone. Sign in later from Me › Settings › Account.

Your account

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Me › Settings › Account › Delete my account, then confirm. It deletes your profile, home area, garage and photos, check-ins, chat, posts, messages and the rest straight away. Events only you host are cancelled, and crews you own pass to another host. It can't be undone. If you can't get into the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

How do I change my name or home area?

Me › Edit profile.

Safety

How do I report someone or something?

Use Report on the post, reply, message, profile or build. Our moderators review reports promptly, normally within 24 hours. You can also email us.

How do I block someone?

Open their profile and tap Block. You won't see their posts, replies or messages, they can't message you, and any connection ends.

Can people see where I am or where I live?

No. Nobody is shown on the map. Your home area is saved to about 1 km and only you can see it. See the Privacy Policy.

Location, notifications and photos

Why does Nightshift want my location?

To show events and places near you, check you in at stops, and guide you turn by turn. Only while the app is open. You can change it in the iPhone's Settings › Nightshift › Location.

I'm not getting reminders

Turn on Me › Settings › Reminders, then allow notifications (or iPhone Settings › Nightshift › Notifications). Send a test reminder checks it works.

A plate wasn't blurred

When you add a photo you can tap to add a blur box anywhere the app missed one. For a photo already saved, open it and check the plates again. If you see someone else's readable plate, report it.

Police and legal requests

Requests from the police or other authorities should be emailed to the address above from an official address. We check every request and disclose data only as the law requires or allows. See the Privacy Policy.
