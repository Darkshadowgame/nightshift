The story

I built Slipstream because finding a meet meant chasing several Facebook groups and Instagram stories, and I still missed the one that had moved.

It started as a meet finder and grew into a home for the whole car and bike community: meets and shows on one map, good roads and photo spots, a garage to show off your build, forums for when something breaks, messages, and directions with warnings from other drivers.

It is free, with no subscription and no tracking. Number plates are blurred on your phone before a photo is uploaded, and home addresses never go on a server. Local meets are never sponsored, and any sponsor is clearly labelled.

I'm one UK enthusiast, not a company, and there are no investors. The app is not on the App Store yet.

Where the money goes

  • Apple developer fee
  • Database and servers
  • Domain

That comes to about £35 a month to launch, and it rises as more people use the app. The fundraiser target is £750 to cover the first year.
