  • Content that several different people report may be hidden automatically until a moderator looks at it. Public text also passes an automatic filter.
  • We review reports promptly, normally within 24 hours. We may remove content, hide it, restrict or suspend an account, or ban someone permanently, without warning where the rule is serious. Abuse, threats, hate, sexual content, anything involving children, doxxing and organising street racing lead to removal and a ban.
