Community Guidelines

Last updated 17 November 2026

Slipstream is for people who love their cars and bikes and want more good meets, not fewer. Meets get banned because of a few people. Don't be them. These rules are part of our Terms of Use and apply to everything: profiles, builds, photos, events, chat, posts, the forum, messages, places and road reports.

The rules

  1. 18+ only. Everyone on Slipstream must be 18 or over. Report anyone who isn't.
  2. No street racing, ever. Don't organise, promote or film racing, speed runs, top-speed pulls, drifting, burnouts, donuts or stunts on public roads. Want to go fast? Book a track day; they're on the map.
  3. Respect meet bans. Don't host or promote a meet inside a PSPO or injunction zone when it applies. The app warns you, but the order is what counts.
  4. Be a good neighbour. No revving, loud exhausts or blocking roads and car parks at meets. Leave the place cleaner than you found it.
  5. No plates. Number plates are blurred on your phone before photos save. Check the blur and fix anything it missed. Never post someone else's plate, address, workplace or where they are.
  6. No harassment. No bullying, threats, intimidation, stalking, or piling on. Disagree about cars all you like; leave people alone.
  7. No hate. No attacks on anyone for race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or age.
  8. Nothing sexual, nothing gory. No nudity, sexual content or graphic violence. Anything involving children is reported to the authorities.
  9. No police spotting. Don't post where police officers or vehicles are. The driving view has one time-limited "Police / speed check" report for drivers on the road; nowhere else.
  10. No dodgy deals. No scams, stolen or illegal parts, or illegal mods offered as road legal. No spam or ads without being a labelled partner.
  11. Be yourself. One account. No pretending to be someone else, and no new accounts to get round a block or ban.
  12. Only post what's yours or what you have permission to use.

Zero tolerance

We don't do warnings for the serious stuff. Abuse, threats, hate, sexual content, anything involving children, doxxing and organising street racing get the content removed and the account banned. Other breaks can mean removal, a time-out or a ban, depending on how serious and how often.

Report and block

  • Report: use Report on the post, reply, message, profile or build (or email us). Pick a reason and add a note if it helps. The person isn't told who reported them.
  • Block: from their profile. You won't see their posts, replies or messages, they can't message you, and any connection ends.
  • Something reported by several people can be hidden straight away until we look. We review reports promptly, normally within 24 hours.
  • If someone is in danger, call 999. For non-emergency crime, call 101.

Appeals

Think we got a decision wrong? Email us from the address on your account and a person will look again.
