Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

The short version

  • No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, no analytics companies, and we never sell your data.
  • Nobody is ever shown on the map. Other people see your builds, posts, crew and level, never where you are or where you live.
  • Your home area is snapped to a grid of about 1 km on your phone before it's saved, and only you can read it.
  • Number plates in your photos are found and blurred on your phone, and the photo is saved without its metadata (including GPS), before anything is uploaded.
  • Your live position is used on your phone and checked by our server when it needs to be (checking in, reporting a road problem). It is not stored and there is no location history.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time in the app: Me › Settings › Account › Delete my account.

Who we are

Slipstream is an app for the UK car and bike meet scene, made by an independent developer in the United Kingdom. For this policy, the developer is the "data controller" ("we", "us").

Two ways to use the app

Demo (no account)

"Try the demo without an account" keeps everything on your phone: the sample people and events, your demo profile, garage and photos. Nothing you make in the demo is sent to our server. The map, place search, routes and nearby photos still use the services listed under Services we use.

Signed in

You sign in with your email address and a one-time code (or a sign-in link). There is no password. Signed in, what you choose to share is stored on our server so other people can see it, as set out below.

Location

  • The app asks for location only while it's open ("While Using the App"). It never tracks you in the background.
  • Your position is used on your phone for the map's blue dot, distances, the driving view and turn-by-turn directions.
  • When you check in at a stop, finish a drive, visit a photo spot or make a road report, your position is sent to our server so it can check you're really there. It is compared and thrown away, not stored.
  • Your home area is set by you (current location or a postcode or town) and snapped to a grid of about 1 km on the phone before it's saved. The exact place is never stored.
  • Nobody, including other users and our moderators, can see where you are or where you live.

Photos and number plates

  • Photos come from your library or camera only when you pick or take them.
  • Before a photo is saved, the app looks for number plates on your phone and pixelates them. You check the boxes and can add or remove them. The saved copy has no metadata (no GPS or camera details). The original never leaves your phone.
  • Photos in your garage, posts and places are visible to signed-in users. Photos in private messages are visible only to the people in that conversation.

Notifications

Reminders (for example, 30 minutes before an event you're going to) are scheduled on your phone as local notifications, if you allow them. The list under the bell lives in the app. We don't use a push notification service yet. If we add one, we'll store a device token so we can send them and will update this policy first.

Our legal bases (UK GDPR)

  • Contract: running your account and the features you use.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the app safe (moderation, reports, rate limits, preventing abuse), security, and fixing bugs.
  • Consent: location, camera, photos and notifications, which you allow or refuse in iOS and can change at any time in the iPhone's Settings.
  • Legal obligation: when the law requires us to keep or disclose something.

Sharing and the police

We don't sell or rent personal data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. We only disclose data to the police, courts or other authorities when the law requires it or clearly allows it, case by case, after checking the request is genuine. Where there is a risk to someone's life we may act quickly. We tell the person affected unless the law or the investigation stops us.

How long we keep it

  • Your account: until you delete it. Deleting it in the app removes, straight away, your profile, home area, garage and photos, check-ins, chat, votes, notices, crew place, posts, messages and road reports. Events only you host are cancelled; crews you own pass to another host. Places you suggested stay, without your name.
  • Road reports: deleted two days after they end.
  • Feedback and moderation records: up to two years, then deleted.
  • Backups: deleted data can stay in our provider's encrypted backups for a short time (normally up to 7 days) before it rolls off.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, or ask us to restrict it. Most of it you can change or delete yourself in the app. To ask for anything else, email us at the address above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), though we'd like the chance to sort it out first.

18+ only

Slipstream is for adults. You must be 18 or over to use it. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it.

Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Every read and write is checked on the server by access rules, so the app can't see what it isn't allowed to. Photo storage is private to signed-in users, message photos to the people in the conversation, and feedback screenshots to our team.

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and use fonts from Google Fonts. They use no cookies and no analytics. GitHub and Google may log your IP address when serving them.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
